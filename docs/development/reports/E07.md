## Interaction envelope

- `AIInteractionEnvelope` records opaque interaction identity, optional null session/turn identifiers, purpose, manifest identity, exact provider/model/config identity, policy/knowledge/safety identity, claim/action ceilings, receipt/proposal identity and status, and ephemeral retention.
- Session and turn identifiers have no behavior; one authorized interaction permits at most one invocation.

## ContextManifest

- `ContextManifest` pins purpose, selected IDs, exact active canonical versions, cutoff, support/counterevidence/unknown roles, exact policy decision identity, provider/model/config identity, context-builder version, and ceilings.
- Manifest and preview identities are deterministic digests of bounded metadata. A selected canonical or policy version change changes the identity or fails the stale-version check.
- The SQLite reader renders content only after provider identity, policy, lineage, preview, and authorization have passed. It never regenerates selected fixture content.

## Provider identity

- Capability is allowlisted by the exact provider, model snapshot, and config snapshot digest.
- The registry separately pins disclosure policy, knowledge and safety snapshots. Unknown, removed, retired, revoked, drifted, or unevaluated identities fail closed before content rendering.
- The offline adapter receives one self-contained request and exposes an invocation counter. It has no storage, network, files, tools, hosted thread, memory, vault reference, or vendor SDK.

## Policy / lineage order

- Each selected record resolves its own accepted `PolicyAxes` together with every parent through `resolve_with_own_policy` before content is read.
- Direct and reconstructive `NEVER_CLOUD`, non-approved processing location, wrong purpose, or expired purpose block before context rendering and invocation.
- The policy decision identity includes exact own/parent policy versions and effective disclosure material.

## Preview / authorization binding

- Preview includes purpose, exact provider/model/config, selected IDs/categories/versions, transformations, policy identity, and ephemeral retention.
- Authorization is opt-in, aware-time bounded, bound to the exact preview, single-use, and consumed before the provider boundary. Material preview drift, expiry, reuse, or a stale canonical version cannot call the provider.

## Provider call-count semantics

- A successful authorization creates one frozen request and invokes the adapter exactly once.
- Provider instructions requesting more context, tools, configuration changes, or another call have no authority. Malformed or unsafe output is rejected without retry or repair.

## Proposal schema

- Every statement has bounded text, one or more selected supporting IDs, explicit uncertainty, and a claim level no higher than `C0_OBSERVATION`.
- The proposal must surface every designated material counterevidence record and selected unknown. Questions are bounded, non-directive, and linked to a selected unknown.
- Initial authority is `PROPOSED` / `proposal_only`. Accept, edit, reject, and discard are bounded in-memory evaluation states and never confer evidence, source-report, accepted-claim, policy, risk, or canonical authority.

## Deterministic validation and safety

- Strict key sets reject malformed/missing/unexpected/oversize fields, duplicate IDs, invented/unselected/wrong-role citations, missing uncertainty/counterevidence/unknowns, unlinked or directive questions, and ceiling violations.
- Deterministic language validation outside the provider rejects the named diagnosis, treatment, therapy, clinical-triage, medication/medical-direction, unsupported-causal, autonomous-recommendation, rescue/monitoring, and relationship/persona families.
- Imported evidence and provider output are data. Instruction-shaped evidence cannot change purpose, selection, provider identity, ceilings, tools, retention, schema, or call count.

## Provider degradation and retention

- Provider unavailable, timeout, malformed output, registry removal, retirement, policy revocation, stale versions, and identity drift do not mutate canonical state.
- Request and raw response remain ephemeral; no provider output table or migration exists. Receipts and ordinary events contain bounded metadata only, never selected content, rendered context, or raw provider output.
- Provider removal leaves accepted archive reads, timeline/explorer behavior, and other local-core dependencies unchanged.

## North Star compatibility

- The envelope preserves optional null session/turn identifiers and exact interaction/context/provider/policy identities for future extension.
- E07 implements no session behavior, guided interviewing, working formulation, skill registry, recommendation, follow-up, voice, clinician workflow, or retrieval engine.

## Migrations / data impact

- No canonical migration or provider-output persistence. One fictional package fixture seeds existing synthetic canonical state in integration tests.
- Added `PyYAML` to the development dependency group because the mandated orchestration validator imports it and failed immediately after `uv sync --frozen` without a declared dependency.
